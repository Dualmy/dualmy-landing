A cycle that doesn't stay on paper

Dualmy doesn't hand you a report and leave. It goes with you from the moment you describe the problem until the improvement is working, and then it looks for the next one.

  1. 1

    Understand

    You tell us what your company does and how it does it: in conversation, with a simple form or by uploading a document you already have.

  2. 2

    Discover

    You describe a challenge: how it works today, what it costs you and what you want to achieve. It's written down, with what the solution must do and a measurable goal.

  3. 3

    Recommend

    We propose a solution using what Dualmy can really do, and before starting you see how much you could save and what it will cost. If something can't be done, we tell you.

  4. 4

    Build

    With one click, Dualmy builds a test app with its processes, tables and connections. You try it with no real-world effects and switch it on when it does what you asked.

  5. 5

    Operate

    The app works with your data, your team and your customers, and you see what happens, how it's going and what it costs.

  6. 6

    Improve

    After 30 days we compare the numbers with the goal. What we observe reveals new opportunities, and the cycle starts again.

What gets built is used every day

Each solution is an app for your team, with its tasks, its data and its alerts. And your customers and suppliers take part without creating an account.

  • One app per solution

    Your team opens Apps and finds their tasks, their data and how everything is going, with no technical screens.

  • Customers and suppliers, no accounts

    They reply by WhatsApp, by email or in a conversation branded with your company. If they have questions, an assistant answers them or hands them to your team.

  • Alerts each person chooses

    New tasks, errors and a daily email summary. Each person decides what they want to receive.

  • Agents that follow the rules

    A step can reason and use tools on its own, with time and cost limits. What can't be undone waits for a person.

It connects to what you already use

You don't have to switch tools or migrate anything. Dualmy works with the email, spreadsheets and systems your company already has, and each connection states what it can do before it's used.

  • Email

    Gmail, Microsoft 365 or your company's mail server: receive, reply and notify.

  • WhatsApp

    Receive orders and send messages, templates and files, with delivery status.

  • Excel

    Read and add rows in the Microsoft 365 Excel workbooks you already work with.

  • Your systems and ERP

    If they have an API, they connect by describing their calls, with no coding or installs.

  • System-to-system events

    Other systems signal when something happens, or a process starts from a form or on a schedule.

  • MCP tools

    Your agents use the tools of an MCP server, only the ones a person reviews and enables.

  • Voice and documents

    Transcribes voice notes and reads Word, PDF, Excel and CSV documents.

  • Your own AI

    Connect your AI account or a model running on your own machines: the cost is yours and you see it at every step.

Anything sent to or written in another system follows the same rules: if it can't be undone, it waits for your approval. Credentials are kept in a secure vault and never reach the AI.
